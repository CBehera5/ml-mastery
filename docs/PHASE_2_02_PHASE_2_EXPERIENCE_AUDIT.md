## Comparison with the founder sample

Retain the sample’s clear hero, generous whitespace, four connected stages, stage-specific blue/teal/purple/amber accents, concrete lab previews and understandable action labels. Simplify its simultaneous search, sign-in, two hero actions, many lab CTAs and full-width upgrade banner. On each screen/state one action receives primary visual weight; other navigation remains quiet and accessible.

The sample is visual reference material, not a commercial/content contract. Do not copy $39, lifetime access, 50+ experiments, lesson/objective counts, a two-minute video, account sync or completed projects without independent approval and evidence. The pictured stage cards imply Project Studio is usable: the specification instead labels it Coming Soon beside the title with no launch action. “Real-world” previews must describe fictional datasets honestly. Introductory MLP may be named truthfully without implying a full deep-learning course.

## Audit limits

This pass sampled one lesson per lab and existing free X-Ray, inspected source-level contracts and used prior scoped mobile evidence. It did not grade every lesson, measure every contrast ratio, run a new mobile matrix, execute Colab, use a screen reader, perform real browser zoom or test with external learners. Those activities have explicit follow-up gates in the content plan and roadmap. No conversion or learning-outcome estimates are asserted.
