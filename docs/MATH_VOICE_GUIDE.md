# Math lesson voice guide

Goal: write like a great Stripe doc crossed with a great teacher. Rigour stays; the system's self-talk goes.

1. **Speak to "you".** Not "the learner". Verbs first: "Drag the dot", not "The dot may be dragged".
2. **Headings promise something.** "Turn a bill into a question", not "Variables and equations".
3. **One idea per paragraph, three sentences at most.** Lead with the point.
4. **Predictions are actions.** Ask a concrete question the reader answers (type, pick, lock in) before the explorer appears.
5. **The playground is the hero.** Big, direct manipulation, the maths updating beside it. Tables and raw grids sit behind a disclosure.
6. **Caveats are one small line, after the delight.** Keep them honest; do not lead with them.
7. **No page self-talk.** Delete sentences that describe the page or the progress system ("Moving a control records activity…"). Progress rules live in one place: the progress disclosure.
8. **Every number in the text is tested** against the maths runtime or real Python.
9. **Story and playground match.** If the story is a bakery, the playground is the bakery, or the text says plainly how the bakery becomes the model.
