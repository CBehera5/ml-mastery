## 4. Learning architecture and teaching depth

Sequence: **P → N → D → V → T → S**. Suggested recalls can skip ahead; no prerequisite route or mastery lock. Practical notebooks revisit prerequisites just in time. A learner may directly attempt independent mastery without doing all guided practice, but must satisfy the same independent requirements.

Each lesson includes, in this order where pedagogically useful:
1. An ML/data question and why the operation exists.
2. Plain-language mental model and visual/table intuition.
3. Terminology and small annotated syntax, with symbols/units connected to Mathematics.
4. A fully worked, bounded example with exact row IDs and inputs.
5. Predict before reveal; change one controlled factor; inspect the trace and explain its effect.
6. Guided repair or code-slot completion with specific feedback.
7. Four objective checks, misconceptions and targeted prerequisite remediation.
8. Notebook section for real execution and a clear statement of what completion does not prove.

The complete lesson-by-lesson design, 144 measurable outcomes and independent check intents are in [the objective matrix](task-013-lesson-objective-matrix.md). No free prose is automatically graded. Reflections may use a constrained claim/reason pair; genuine open explanations remain ungraded learning prompts.
